Step 1) Is your PC setup: can you list devices over novacom?
- Yes: Go to Step 2
- No: - Install Java, Install Drivers, Do novacom -l from command line

Step 2) Is your device activated and healthy? Or do you need to activate, reset or upgrade?
- Healthy: Go to Step 5
- Activate/Reset: Go to Step 3

Step 3) Identify your device:
- Old Phones: Pre, Pre Plus, Pixi, Pixi Plus
	- Reset: How to use Doctor (with filenames)
	- Activate: Go to Step 4
- Later Phones: Pre2, Pre3, Veer
	- Reset or Upgrade: How to use Doctor (with filenames)
	- Activate: Go to Step 4
- Touchpad 10” (WiFi or 4G)
	- Reset to Stock: How to use Doctor (with filenames), then Step 4
	- Upgrade to 3.1.0: How to use Doctor (with filenames), NO Step 4-5
-  Touchpad Go 8” (4G)
	- Reset to Stock: How to use Doctor (with filenames), then Step 4

Step 4) Activate with deviceTool
	- use deviceTool from Command Line, then Step 5

Step 5) Install App Stores
	- install Preware from command line
	- Install App Catalog from command line
	- Go to Step 6-type variant

Step 6 Old Phones)
	- No modern internet, HTTP only, Classic for PalmOS, lots of games to play!

Step 6 Later Phones)
	- Modern HTTPS through Preware
	- Retro browser rendering limitations
	- Many games, some functional apps (Podcasts, etc…)

Step 6 Touchpad Go)
	- Modern HTTPS through Preware
	- 2018-era browser in Preware
	- USB, Bluetooth game controllers, many games are compatible (some are not), almost all Touchpad apps work on Go

Step 6 Touchpad 10”)
	- Modern HTTPS pre-installed
	- Modern browsers available
	- Tons of games, including ports from Pre, Linux, and iOS being made by community
	- Tons of apps, new and restored apps from community

	- Porting guides
