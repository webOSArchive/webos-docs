Join the Community

There's still an active webOS Community, swapping notes on devices and patches, and even building new apps...

  • Discord + SimpleChat: the most active community of webOS Users is on a modern webOS app called SimpleChat which is also connected to a Discord server. Join in the conversation from both old and new devices!

  • Bluesky: webOS Archive has officially eXited Twitter, but you can follow us on Bluesky.
     

  • Fediverse + RSS: webOS Archive is on Mastodon, thanks to a community-hosted server. On webOS, you can also follow via RSS with apps like FeedSpider.
     

  • Forums

    • Legacy: In late 2022, the community learned about the sunsetting of the webOS Nation Forums. Much of this important historical content has been archived by the Wayback Machine (also as an archive!) A handy shortcut can be found at forums.webosarchive.org
       

    • Re-birth: In 2023, the community started creating a new Forum, which is evolving via a steering group on Discord. Join the new webOS Lives Forums!
